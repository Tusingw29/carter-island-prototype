      Walking is quiet, running is loud, crouching is silent. A guard's flashlight is what he can see.
      Stand still in a pitch-dark patch and nobody can see you. Seen is not caught: a guard who
      spots you freezes for a moment, then chases, and he is slower than your run. He has to wind
      up a lunge to grab you, so run when he crouches. Out of his sight for two seconds and he
      loses you. If he gets close, shove him and run. A thrown cup stuns a guard if it hits his
      head, and if it misses he walks over to where it landed. Knocked-out guards wake up after a
      while, and every guard calls in on the radio once a minute: a missed call brings someone to look.
